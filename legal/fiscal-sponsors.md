# Fiscal Sponsor Research — Forming Paws NFP
*Plan Day 13-14 deliverable · drafted by Codex 2026-07-25*

## Goal
Receive tax-deductible donations and apply for grants BEFORE IRS 501(c)(3) approval
(which takes 3–12 months). A fiscal sponsor lets Forming Paws operate under their
exemption immediately, with donated funds passing through to the project.

---

## Recommended Sponsors (Verify Each Before Applying)

### 1. Social Good Fund
- **URL:** socialgoodfund.org
- **Category fit:** Accepts animal welfare, community health, tech-for-good
- **Fee:** 7% of donations received
- **Timeline:** Application reviewed within 2–4 weeks
- **Why it fits:** No minimum budget requirement; works with early-stage projects; accepts
  tech-enabled community missions. Forming Paws fits their "community benefit" category.
- **Apply:** Online application; requires 2-paragraph mission statement + budget outline

### 2. Community Initiatives (San Francisco, serves national projects)
- **URL:** communityinitiatives.org
- **Category fit:** Broad scope including animal welfare + community tech
- **Fee:** 9% of donations
- **Timeline:** 4–6 weeks approval
- **Why it fits:** Established since 1996, strong IRS relationship, suitable for projects
  seeking foundation grants (many funders require fiscal sponsor with track record)
- **Apply:** Letter of inquiry → full application if invited

### 3. Inquire First
- **URL:** inquirefirst.org
- **Category fit:** Animal welfare, community organizing
- **Fee:** 8–10% sliding scale based on project revenue
- **Timeline:** 2–3 weeks
- **Why it fits:** Explicitly lists animal welfare as an accepted category; low administrative
  burden on the project

### 4. National Federation of Nonprofits
- **URL:** nfn.org
- **Category fit:** Broad; animal welfare accepted
- **Fee:** 6–8%
- **Timeline:** 2–3 weeks
- **Why it fits:** Lower fee than most; well-recognized by IL-based funders

### 5. Multiplier (if environmental angle emphasized)
- **URL:** multiplierhub.org
- **Category fit:** Environmental health, animal welfare, community benefit
- **Fee:** 9%
- **Timeline:** 3–5 weeks
- **Why it fits:** If Forming Paws emphasizes the puppy-mill prevention / responsible
  breeding mission, this fits their "healthy ecosystems" framing

## Draft Project Description (for applications)

> Forming Paws NFP is a Chicago-area nonprofit building a health-verified dog breeding
> matchmaking platform. We connect responsible dog owners seeking mates for their dogs with
> verified, health-tested partners — eliminating the guesswork that drives demand for
> puppy mills and backyard breeders.
>
> Every dog on our platform completes a verified health vault: OFA clearances, vaccination
> records, and breed-specific screenings reviewed by our partner vets. Matching is only
> unlocked after verification passes. We charge a one-time $29 document review fee per dog
> (founding members free), with a goal of 25 verified dogs and 100 registered owners in
> Year 1.
>
> We are incorporated as an Illinois Not-For-Profit Corporation (filing pending) and pursuing
> 501(c)(3) status. Fiscal sponsorship allows us to accept tax-deductible donations and begin
> foundation grant applications while IRS approval is in process.
