## ARTICLE I — NAME AND OFFICES

**Section 1.1 — Name.** The name of the corporation is Forming Paws NFP (the "Corporation").

**Section 1.2 — Principal Office.** The principal office of the Corporation shall be located at such place as the Board of Directors may designate. The initial principal office is located in [CITY], Illinois.

**Section 1.3 — Registered Agent.** The Corporation shall maintain a registered agent in the State of Illinois as required by law.

---

## ARTICLE II — PURPOSES AND POWERS

**Section 2.1 — Purposes.** The Corporation is organized exclusively for charitable, educational, and scientific purposes, as set forth in the Articles of Incorporation.

**Section 2.2 — Powers.** The Corporation shall have the power to do all acts and things necessary, suitable, convenient, or proper for the accomplishment of its purposes, not inconsistent with Illinois law or Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code.

---

## ARTICLE III — MEMBERS

**Section 3.1 — No Members.** The Corporation shall have no members as defined under the Illinois General Not For Profit Corporation Act. All governance authority rests with the Board of Directors.

---

## ARTICLE IV — BOARD OF DIRECTORS

**Section 4.1 — Authority.** The business and affairs of the Corporation shall be managed by and under the direction of the Board of Directors.

**Section 4.2 — Number.** The Board of Directors shall consist of not fewer than **three (3)** and not more than **nine (9)** directors. The initial Board shall consist of three directors.

**Section 4.3 — Term.** Each director shall serve a term of **two (2) years** and may be re-elected without limit.

**Section 4.4 — Election.** Directors shall be elected by the Board of Directors at the annual meeting of the Board.

**Section 4.5 — Vacancies.** Any vacancy occurring on the Board of Directors may be filled by the affirmative vote of a majority of the remaining directors.

**Section 4.6 — Removal.** Any director may be removed, with or without cause, by a two-thirds (2/3) vote of the entire Board of Directors at any regular or special meeting.

**Section 4.7 — Resignation.** Any director may resign at any time by delivering written notice to the Chairperson or the Secretary of the Board.

**Section 4.8 — Compensation.** Directors shall serve without compensation, except that they may be reimbursed for reasonable expenses incurred in connection with their service to the Corporation, as approved by the Board.

**Section 4.9 — Conflict of Interest.** Directors shall disclose any material interest in a transaction or matter before the Board. A conflicted director shall recuse themselves from the vote. The Corporation shall maintain a Conflict of Interest Policy consistent with IRS requirements for 501(c)(3) organizations.

---

## ARTICLE V — MEETINGS OF THE BOARD

**Section 5.1 — Annual Meeting.** The Board of Directors shall hold an annual meeting each year at such time and place as the Chairperson designates.

**Section 5.2 — Regular Meetings.** The Board may hold regular meetings at such times as it shall determine by resolution.

**Section 5.3 — Special Meetings.** Special meetings of the Board may be called by the Chairperson or by any two directors. Notice stating the time, place, and purpose of any special meeting shall be given at least **48 hours** in advance.

**Section 5.4 — Quorum.** A majority of the total number of directors then in office shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

**Section 5.5 — Voting.** Each director shall have one vote. Except as otherwise required by law or these Bylaws, all matters shall be decided by a majority vote of directors present at a meeting at which a quorum is present.

**Section 5.6 — Action Without Meeting.** Any action required or permitted to be taken at a meeting of the Board may be taken without a meeting if all directors consent in writing. Written consent may be transmitted by email.

**Section 5.7 — Remote Participation.** Directors may participate in meetings via telephone, video conference, or other electronic means through which all participants can hear and communicate with each other. Such participation constitutes presence in person at the meeting.

---

## ARTICLE VI — OFFICERS

**Section 6.1 — Officers.** The officers of the Corporation shall be: Chairperson, Vice Chairperson, Secretary, and Treasurer. The Board may create additional officer positions.

**Section 6.2 — Election and Term.** Officers shall be elected by the Board of Directors at the annual meeting. Each officer shall serve a term of one (1) year or until their successor is elected.

**Section 6.3 — Chairperson.** The Chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the Board, serve as the chief executive officer of the Corporation, and perform all duties customarily incident to the office.

**Section 6.4 — Vice Chairperson.** The Vice Chairperson shall perform the duties of the Chairperson in the Chairperson's absence or incapacity.

**Section 6.5 — Secretary.** The Secretary shall keep the minutes of all Board meetings, maintain all corporate records, give notices required by law or these Bylaws, and perform other duties as assigned by the Board.

**Section 6.6 — Treasurer.** The Treasurer shall have charge of the corporate funds and financial records, ensure proper financial controls are maintained, and report on the financial condition of the Corporation at each annual meeting. The Corporation shall not require dual signatures for checks below $[AMOUNT — suggest $500], but shall require two authorized signatures for disbursements above that threshold.

---

## ARTICLE VII — COMMITTEES

**Section 7.1 — Standing Committees.** The Board may establish standing committees by resolution, including but not limited to: Finance Committee, Governance Committee, and Program Committee. Each committee shall have at least two Board members.

**Section 7.2 — Authority.** Committees may make recommendations to the Board but may not bind the Corporation unless expressly authorized by the Board.

---

## ARTICLE VIII — FISCAL MATTERS

**Section 8.1 — Fiscal Year.** The fiscal year of the Corporation shall end on **December 31** of each year.

**Section 8.2 — Bank Accounts.** The Corporation shall maintain accounts at federally insured financial institutions. Account signatories shall be designated by the Board.

**Section 8.3 — Contracts and Expenditures.** The Board shall authorize all contracts and expenditures exceeding $[AMOUNT — suggest $1,000] by resolution.

**Section 8.4 — Annual Review.** The Board shall review the financial statements of the Corporation no less than annually. Once annual revenue exceeds $250,000, the Board shall commission an independent audit.

**Section 8.5 — Public Inspection.** The Corporation shall make its IRS Form 990, Articles of Incorporation, and these Bylaws available for public inspection as required by law.

---

## ARTICLE IX — INDEMNIFICATION

The Corporation shall indemnify its directors, officers, and agents to the fullest extent permitted by Illinois law and consistent with Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code. The Corporation shall not indemnify any person for acts of willful misconduct or gross negligence.

---

## ARTICLE X — AMENDMENTS

These Bylaws may be amended or repealed, and new Bylaws adopted, by the affirmative vote of two-thirds (2/3) of the entire Board of Directors at any regular or special meeting, provided that written notice of the proposed amendment has been delivered to each director at least **ten (10) days** before the meeting.

---

## ARTICLE XI — DISSOLUTION

Upon dissolution of the Corporation, and after paying or adequately providing for the debts and obligations of the Corporation, all remaining assets shall be distributed to one or more organizations exempt under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code that carry out purposes similar to the Corporation, as determined by the Board and consistent with the Articles of Incorporation.
